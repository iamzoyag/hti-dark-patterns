Decision-Making in AI-Assisted Resource Allocation

Human-Technology Interaction Lab & AI Systems

Before you begin

Informed Consent

You are being invited to participate in a research study examining how humans interact with AI assistants during complex decision-making tasks. The study takes approximately 15–25 minutes.

What you will do

You will complete one hands-on marketing task — either allocating a budget across channels to maximize ROI, or configuring a social media launch post to maximize engagement — using sliders and toggles while adhering to live constraints and dynamic updates. Throughout the task, you will interact with an AI Marketing Advisor via chat to help guide your strategy.

Data collection

All interface interactions (such as slider adjustments, clicks) and chat messages will be logged for research purposes. No personally identifiable information beyond your participant ID will be stored. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.

Risks and benefits

There are no foreseeable risks beyond those encountered in daily computer use. You will contribute to ongoing academic research regarding human-AI collaboration and system reliability under varying levels of task complexity.

Before you begin

Informed Consent

This research study (~15-25 minutes) examines how people interact with an AI assistant during decision-making tasks. You'll complete a hands-on task while chatting with an AI assistant for guidance; some trials include a secondary "Divided Attention" panel — click "Match" whenever it shows 5 (≥70% accuracy qualifies you for a completion bonus). At the end of the study, you'll see a summary of how your choices compared to the best possible outcome across all rounds. All interface interactions and chat messages are logged for research purposes; no personal information beyond a participant ID is stored, and you may withdraw at any time without penalty.
